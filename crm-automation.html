What CRM automation means

A CRM stores your customers and sales conversations, but it is only useful when it is up to date. CRM automation does the routine updates for you. When something happens, such as a new form enquiry, the right record is created or changed and the next step starts on its own.

What we can automate

  • Lead capture: website forms, chats and calls go straight into your CRM.
  • Lead qualification: AI asks the right questions and flags serious enquiries.
  • Follow-ups: emails, texts or WhatsApp messages go out on a schedule you approve.
  • Task reminders: your team is told who to call next and why.
  • Record updates: AI can summarise a conversation and save it on the contact.
  • Reports: a simple view of new leads and where deals are stuck.

Which CRMs we work with

We work with common CRMs such as HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Salesforce, and we can often connect other tools through their API. If you do not have a CRM yet, we will suggest a simple one that fits your size. See how we connect business tools.

How a project runs

  1. Map the journey. We follow one lead from first contact to sale and find the manual steps.
  2. Clean up the basics. We agree the fields and stages your team will really use.
  3. Build and test. We build each automation and test it with sample leads.
  4. Launch and support. We watch it closely at first and improve it over time.

A simple example

Imagine a new enquiry arrives from your website. With CRM automation, a contact is created, the source is recorded and the customer receives a friendly reply. A team member gets a task to call them. If the customer does not respond, a polite follow-up goes out two days later. When they reply, the follow-up stops and the deal moves to the next stage.

None of those steps is difficult. The value comes from having them happen every time, even during your busiest week.

Signs you need CRM automation

  • Enquiries sit unanswered in several inboxes
  • You rely on memory or spreadsheets for follow-ups
  • Staff type the same details into more than one system
  • You cannot easily see where each deal stands
  • Old customers never hear from you again

Keeping your data clean

Automation makes bad data travel faster, so we set simple rules first. We decide which fields are required, how to avoid duplicates, and who owns each stage. We also test with sample contacts before touching real records, and agree how to back up important data.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to change my CRM?

Usually not. We start with what you already use and only suggest a change if it is holding you back.

What is the first thing I should automate?

Start with new lead capture and the first reply. It is simple, and it helps stop enquiries from being forgotten.

Will automated follow-ups feel spammy?

They should not. We keep messages short and relevant, stop them when a customer replies, and you approve the wording first.

Is my customer data safe?

We agree what data is used and who can access it, sign an NDA before we start, and give you ownership of what we build.
