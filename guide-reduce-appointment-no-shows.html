How to Reduce Appointment No-Shows: 7 Steps That Work

No-shows leave empty chairs, idle staff and lost income. The fixes are simple, and some are backed by solid research. Here are seven steps you can start this week.

By NextGen AI Labs · Updated 29 October 2026

In this guide
  1. 1. Send text reminders
  2. 2. Word the reminder carefully
  3. 3. Make rescheduling one tap
  4. 4. Keep a waitlist
  5. 5. Confirm at booking
  6. 6. Take a small deposit for long appointments
  7. 7. Automate all of it

1. Send text reminders

Reminders work. A Cochrane review of randomized trials found that 78.6% of people attended when they received a text reminder, compared with 67.8% without one. Send one reminder the day before and, for longer gaps, another a few days ahead.

2. Word the reminder carefully

The wording matters. In a trial across 14 hospitals, a better-worded reminder cut missed appointments from 21.1% to 14.2%. Be clear and friendly, include the date, time and place, and mention that the slot was reserved for them.

3. Make rescheduling one tap

Many no-shows are people who could not come and did not want to call. Let them reply “2 to reschedule” or tap a link to pick a new time. A rescheduled appointment is far better than an empty one.

4. Keep a waitlist

When someone cancels, offer the slot automatically to people on a waitlist. This turns a cancellation into a booking instead of lost time.

5. Confirm at booking

Send an instant confirmation with all the details and an “add to calendar” link. People who put the appointment in their own calendar are more likely to remember it.

6. Take a small deposit for long appointments

For long or high-value appointments, a small deposit reduces no-shows. Make the policy clear when people book.

7. Automate all of it

Reminders, rescheduling and waitlists can run on their own. Our booking assistant for salons and AI front desk for clinics do exactly this, so your team stops spending hours on confirmation calls.

Frequently asked questions

When should I send appointment reminders?

A reminder the day before works well for most businesses, with an extra reminder a few days ahead for appointments booked weeks in advance.

Do reminders annoy customers?

Most people appreciate one or two clear, friendly reminders, especially when they can reschedule with one tap.
