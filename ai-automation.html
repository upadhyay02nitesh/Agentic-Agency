    WHAT WE BUILD

    Six kinds of AI that pay for themselves

    Chat & voice agents

    AI agents on WhatsApp, your website and phone lines that answer questions, qualify leads and book appointments in English and other major languages, with a clean handoff to your team.

    Assistants on your documents

    Ask questions of your policies, product manuals, contracts or SOPs and get answers with the source cited. Built with retrieval (RAG) on data you control.

    Document AI

    Read invoices, purchase orders, KYC documents, forms and handwritten slips. Extract fields, validate them and push them into your systems.

    Workflow automation

    Multi-step workflows across CRM, email, sheets, payments and ERP using n8n, Make, Zapier or custom code, with retries, alerts and logs.

    AI inside your product

    Smart search, recommendations, summaries, auto-tagging and copilots added to your web or mobile app.

    Ask-your-data analytics

    Dashboards plus a plain-language question box: “How did New York sales last month compare with March?” answered from your live data.

    RESPONSIBLE AI

    AI you can trust in front of customers

    Most AI projects fail on reliability, not on the model. This is how we build ours.

    Human in the loop

    Approvals for money, refunds and anything sensitive. The AI drafts, a person decides.

    Your data stays yours

    Business-tier model APIs that do not train on your data, access controls, and handling aligned with GDPR and CCPA.

    Tested before launch

    We test against a set of real questions and edge cases and share the pass rate before going live.

    Every action logged

    Conversations, tool calls and decisions are logged so you can audit what happened and why.

    Clear limits

    The agent only does the tasks it is allowed to, says “I don’t know” instead of guessing, and hands off when it should.

    Cost under control

    Right-sized models, caching and usage caps so the monthly AI bill is predictable.
