AI Chatbot for a Small Business Website: A Plain-English Guide

An AI chatbot answers your website visitors’ questions instantly, day and night, and turns them into enquiries or bookings. Here is how it works in plain English, and how to launch one without the usual mistakes.

By NextGen AI Labs · Updated 29 October 2026

In this guide
  1. What it can do
  2. What it should not do
  3. How it learns your business
  4. What it costs
  5. How to launch it safely

What it can do

  • Answer questions about your services, prices, opening hours and policies.
  • Collect a visitor’s name, email and phone number so you can follow up.
  • Book appointments or suggest the right next step.
  • Hand the conversation to a person when it is needed.

What it should not do

A good chatbot knows its limits. It should not invent prices or promises, give medical, legal or financial advice, or argue with customers. When it is unsure, it should say so and pass the conversation to your team.

How it learns your business

The chatbot is given your own information: services, prices, FAQs, policies and opening hours. It answers from that information, and you can see and approve what it knows. When something changes, the information is updated and the answers change with it.

What it costs

Costs usually include a one-time setup and a small monthly plan for hosting, usage and updates. Our AI Chat Assistant is a fixed setup price plus a monthly plan with a set number of chats included, agreed in writing before you pay anything.

How to launch it safely

  1. Start with your 20 most common questions.
  2. Test it with real examples before it goes live.
  3. Always offer a “talk to a person” option.
  4. Review the first few weeks of conversations and improve the answers.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new website to add an AI chatbot?

No. A chatbot can be added to almost any existing website with a small piece of code.

Can the chatbot also answer on WhatsApp or Instagram?

Yes. The same assistant can answer on your website, WhatsApp and Instagram so customers get the same quick answers everywhere.
