How Much Does an AI Receptionist Cost for a Small Business?

An AI receptionist answers your phone, books appointments and sends you a summary of every call. Here is what it costs, what you get for the money, and a simple way to check whether it pays for itself.

By NextGen AI Labs · Updated 29 October 2026

In this guide
  1. What you actually pay for
  2. Typical price ranges
  3. How to check if it pays for itself
  4. What to ask before you buy
  5. The bottom line

What you actually pay for

Almost every AI receptionist has three parts to its price:

  • Setup: teaching the AI your services, prices, opening hours and rules, connecting it to your calendar and phone number, and testing it with real call examples.
  • A monthly plan: hosting, monitoring, updates when your hours or prices change, and fixing anything that stops working.
  • Usage: the phone line and AI processing for each minute of calls. Good plans include a set number of minutes so your bill is predictable.

Typical price ranges

Prices vary with how many calls you get and how much the AI needs to do. A simple “answer questions and take a message” setup costs much less than one that books into your calendar, takes deposits and transfers urgent calls. As a reference, our AI Receptionist package starts at a one-time setup plus a fixed monthly plan that includes call minutes and maintenance, with the full price agreed in writing before any work starts.

How to check if it pays for itself

Use three numbers from your own business:

  1. How many calls you miss in a typical week (check your phone’s call log).
  2. How many of those callers would have become customers.
  3. What one new customer is worth to you.

If you miss 10 calls a week, two of those would have booked, and each customer is worth $200, missed calls cost you about $1,700 a month. Even winning back a fraction of that usually covers an AI receptionist. You can try your own numbers in our savings calculator.

What to ask before you buy

  • Does it book straight into my calendar, or just take messages?
  • Can it transfer urgent calls to me, and can I set the rules?
  • How many call minutes are included, and what happens if I go over?
  • Who updates it when my prices, hours or services change?
  • Do I own my data, and is it kept private?

The bottom line

An AI receptionist is not a replacement for great staff. It is a safety net that makes sure every caller gets answered, especially after hours and when your team is busy. Start with one clear job, such as answering and booking, measure the calls it handles, then decide whether to expand.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI receptionist really sound natural?

Modern voice AI sounds natural for everyday conversations like bookings and opening hours. We recommend telling callers they are speaking with an AI assistant, and always letting them ask for a person.

Will I need a new phone number?

Usually not. Most setups forward your existing number to the AI when you are busy or after hours, so customers keep calling the number they know.
