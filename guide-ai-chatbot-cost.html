How Much Does an AI Chatbot Cost for a Small Business?

The price of an AI chatbot depends on what it needs to do. Here is what you pay for, what pushes the price up, and how to compare quotes fairly.

By NextGen AI Labs · Updated 31 October 2026

In this guide
  1. What you are paying for
  2. What changes the price
  3. Our chatbot prices
  4. DIY tool or done-for-you service?
  5. Questions to ask before you buy
  6. How to compare two quotes
  7. Is it worth paying for?

What you are paying for

  • Setup: planning the chatbot, training it on your business, connecting your tools and testing it.
  • AI usage: the running cost of each conversation, usually covered up to a monthly limit.
  • Hosting and support: keeping it online, fixing problems and updating its information.

What changes the price

  • How many questions and topics it must cover
  • Whether it answers only or also takes actions such as booking
  • How many channels it uses, such as your website and WhatsApp
  • How many tools it connects to
  • How many conversations you expect each month
  • How many languages it needs

DIY tool or done-for-you service?

A DIY chatbot tool can be a sensible low-cost start if you have the time to set it up and keep it updated. A done-for-you service costs more upfront, but someone else handles the training, connections and fixes. Compare the total cost, including your own time.

Questions to ask before you buy

  • What is included in the monthly fee?
  • What happens if we go over the usage limit?
  • Who updates the chatbot when our prices or hours change?
  • Can customers reach a real person?
  • Who owns the content and the conversation data?
  • Is there a minimum contract?

How to compare two quotes

A low price may leave out work you will need later, while a high price may include things you will never use. Put each quote into the same simple table: setup cost, monthly cost, included conversations, channels, connected tools, update policy and contract length. Add up the total for the first year. That number is far more useful than the headline price.

Is it worth paying for?

Do a quick estimate. Count the enquiries you receive after hours each month, then work out how many might turn into customers if they got an instant reply. Add the staff time spent answering the same questions. If those figures comfortably cover the monthly cost, it is worth testing. If you get very few enquiries, improve your website and marketing first.

Frequently asked questions

Are there any free AI chatbots?

Some tools have free plans, usually with limits on features or conversations. They can be fine for testing, but check the limits before relying on one.

Why is there a monthly fee?

A chatbot has ongoing costs for AI usage and hosting, and it needs updates as your business changes.
