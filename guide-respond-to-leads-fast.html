Why Replying to Leads Within Minutes Wins More Customers

When someone fills in your form or messages you, they are often contacting other businesses too. Whoever replies first usually gets the conversation. Here is what the research says and how to be first, every time.

By NextGen AI Labs · Updated 29 October 2026

In this guide
  1. What the research says
  2. Why speed matters so much
  3. How to reply fast without living on your phone
  4. Where AI fits in

What the research says

A Harvard Business Review study audited 2,241 US companies and found that firms which contacted a new lead within an hour were nearly seven times more likely to have a real conversation with a decision-maker than firms that waited even one hour longer. The same research found that many companies took far longer than that, or never replied at all.

Why speed matters so much

A new lead is at their most interested in the minutes after they reach out. Wait a few hours and they have moved on, booked with someone else or simply forgotten. A fast, friendly reply also signals that you are organised and reliable.

How to reply fast without living on your phone

  • Bring every lead into one place: website forms, ads, WhatsApp, email and social messages.
  • Send an instant, personal first reply that answers the question and suggests a next step, such as a call or a booking.
  • Follow up automatically a day or two later if they do not reply.
  • Pass hot leads to a person straight away with the full conversation.

Where AI fits in

An AI assistant can do the first reply in about a minute, in your tone, at any hour, then hand over to you when the lead is ready to talk. That is exactly what our Lead Follow-up Engine does, and it is how real estate agents stay first even while they are showing a property.

Frequently asked questions

How fast should I reply to a new lead?

As fast as you can: within minutes is ideal, and within an hour should be the minimum. Automation makes minutes realistic even after hours.

Will automatic replies feel robotic?

Not if they are written well. Good AI replies answer the actual question in your tone and always offer a way to talk to a person.
