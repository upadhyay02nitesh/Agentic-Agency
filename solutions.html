In plain words

What is an AI employee?

Think of a new team member who never sleeps, never forgets to follow up and learns your business in days. It works in three simple steps.

  1. 1

    It listens

    Wherever customers reach you: phone calls, website chat, WhatsApp, Instagram, email or forms.

  2. 2

    It knows your business

    We teach it your services, prices, hours and rules, the same way you would train a new hire.

  3. 3

    It acts, then tells you

    It replies, books, follows up and updates your records, then sends you a short summary. Anything unusual goes straight to you.

A new staff member
  • Weeks to train
  • Works set hours
  • Can miss calls when busy
An AI employee
  • Ready in days
  • Works 24/7, in many languages
  • Answers every call and message

It doesn’t replace your team. It takes the repetitive work off their plate, so they can spend more time with customers.
