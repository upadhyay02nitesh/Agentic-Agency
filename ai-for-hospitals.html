What the research says

Why this works

  • In a trial across 14 hospitals, better-worded text reminders cut missed appointments from 21.1% to 14.2%.

    Source: Berliner Senderey et al., npj Digital Medicine (2020)

  • Across randomized trials, 78.6% of people attended appointments when they got a text reminder, versus 67.8% without one.

    Source: Cochrane systematic review of mobile phone reminders

Published research from healthcare and sales. It shows the pattern; results in your business will differ, and we measure them with you.
