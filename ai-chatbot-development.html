What an AI chatbot does for a business

A good AI chatbot works like a well-trained front desk. It answers the questions your team hears every day, such as prices, opening hours, services and availability. It also collects the details you need from each new enquiry, then passes them to you.

Older chatbots followed fixed scripts and gave up quickly. An AI chatbot reads what the customer actually typed, so people can ask in their own words. It only answers from the information you approve.

What we build

  • Website chatbots that answer questions and capture leads.
  • WhatsApp and messaging assistants for customers who prefer to message you.
  • Booking assistants that check availability and book appointments.
  • Document-trained assistants that answer from your own policies, menus or price lists.
  • Handover to people, so a customer can reach your team whenever the chatbot should not answer.

How AI chatbot development works

  1. Free consultation. We look at the questions you get most often and where enquiries are lost.
  2. Written plan. You get the scope and a fixed price before any work starts.
  3. Build and train. We train the chatbot on your business and connect it to the tools you already use.
  4. Test, launch and improve. You test it first. After launch we use real conversations to improve its answers.

Who it suits

A chatbot is a strong first step if your team repeats the same answers, you get enquiries after hours, or website visitors leave without contacting you. It works for clinics, salons, restaurants, real estate and many other industries. You can try our scripted demo to see how a conversation feels.

What makes a chatbot useful

The AI model is only part of the result. What matters most is the information it is given and the rules around it. A useful chatbot has accurate answers about your services, clear limits on what it may say, and a simple way to reach a person.

It should also sound like your business. A dental clinic needs a calm and careful tone, while a beach resort can be warmer and more relaxed. We agree the tone with you, then test it with the questions your customers actually ask.

What we need from you

  • Your services, prices and opening hours
  • The questions customers ask most often, and your best answers
  • Your rules, such as what the chatbot must never promise
  • Who should receive enquiries and urgent messages
  • Access to the website or tools we are connecting

If this information is scattered across emails and documents, that is fine. We will help you turn it into a clean set of answers.

Chatbot, FAQ page or live chat?

An FAQ page is free to keep, but customers must search it themselves. Live chat feels personal, but it needs someone available. An AI chatbot answers immediately at any hour and can pass harder questions to a person. Many businesses use all three together: the FAQ for simple facts, the chatbot for instant replies and people for complicated conversations.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build an AI chatbot?

It depends on the scope. AI Starter is usually live in 3 to 5 days and the AI Chat Assistant in 7 to 10 days. Your written plan confirms the timing.

Will the chatbot make up answers?

We train it on information you approve and tell it to hand the conversation to a person when it is unsure. No AI is perfect, so we test it before launch and review real conversations afterwards.

Can it work on WhatsApp as well as my website?

Yes. We can connect it to your website and to messaging channels such as WhatsApp. Availability can depend on the channel, and we confirm this in your plan.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We set it up, train it and connect it. You only need to tell us about your business and test the result.
