SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

Security practices built into delivery

What we do on every engagement, and what we arrange when your tender needs more.

On every project

  • NDA before discovery, IP assignment to you on payment
  • Personal data handled in line with GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA requirements
  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Role-based access, least privilege, MFA on all admin accounts
  • Code review and OWASP Top 10 checks before release
  • Secrets in a vault, never in code
  • Automated backups and a tested restore plan
  • Audit logs for admin and data actions

When your tender requires more

  • Independent penetration testing by an accredited third-party firm
  • Hosting in your region (AWS, Azure or Google Cloud in the US, EU, UK or elsewhere) for data residency
  • Alignment with ISO 27001 / SOC 2 controls in your environment
  • Accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA (ADA / EN 301 549)
  • Signed data processing agreement
  • Completion of your security questionnaire

We only list certifications we actually hold. Where a tender asks for a certified audit, we name the third-party auditor in our proposal.
